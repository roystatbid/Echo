Echo

Sonar, using nothing but the speaker and microphone you already have.

  1. The iPad chirps. The chirp bounces off whatever is in front of it.
  2. The microphone hears the echo a few milliseconds later. Distance is just the speed of sound times half that delay.
  3. There is only one microphone, so it can't tell which way an echo came from. Turning slowly on the spot is what paints the room: the motion sensors supply the bearing.

Needs microphone and motion access. Turn the volume up, and take headphones out. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere — audio never leaves the device.
